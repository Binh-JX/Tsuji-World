**Artist:** 辻司 --- Tsukasa Tsuji\
**Born:** 20 December 1933\

## 0. PURPOSE AND STATUS

This document is the master project specification and source of truth
for the creative, archival, content, and technical direction of **TSUJI
WORLD**. It consolidates the Digital Art Book Master Blueprint, the
family/material collection task assignment, and the Architecture Master
Document.

The primary V1 positioning is **Digital Art Book & Living Archive**. The
larger digital-museum concept remains the long-term direction. A future
physical museum is possible but is not a promise or a V1 requirement.

This document describes project direction, not automatically verified
art-history facts. Any date, title, award, exhibition, quotation,
provenance, ownership detail, or interpretation that has not been
verified must be marked accordingly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. CORE CONCEPT

TSUJI WORLD combines five ideas:

### 01 --- DIGITAL ART BOOK

A premium, cinematic, editorial presentation of selected paintings and
the artist's journey. The experience should feel like entering a
high-quality art book rather than browsing a conventional artist
website.

### 02 --- LIVING ARCHIVE

A structured archive that can grow as the family discovers, photographs,
scans, identifies, verifies, and documents more works and historical
material.

### 03 --- ARTISTIC JOURNEY

Visitors should experience the evolution of a long artistic life rather
than simply read a biography.

### 04 --- FAMILY MEMORY

The paintings are not only museum objects. They have lived inside a
family home, on walls and in rooms, alongside everyday family life.

### 05 --- FUTURE MUSEUM

The digital project can become an early home for the collection while
the family considers a dedicated physical museum in the future.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. CENTRAL STORY

The central narrative is:

> **More than seventy years of painting eventually became too much for
> one house to contain.**

The home has limited space. Many paintings are large. The family has
lived with them for decades. The collection continues to exist alongside
public exhibitions and future exhibition plans.

The resulting narrative is:

**Paintings were created over a lifetime.**\
↓\
**They filled a home.**\
↓\
**The home became an archive.**\
↓\
**The archive became a digital art book and living archive.**\
↓\
**The digital world may grow into a digital museum.**\
↓\
**Perhaps one day it may support a physical museum.**

The physical museum is a future chapter, not a marketing promise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. POSITIONING

TSUJI WORLD is **not**:

-   a commercial artist website;
-   an online art shop;
-   a generic portfolio;
-   a corporate museum website;
-   a conventional biography website;
-   a gallery catalogue dumped onto the web;
-   a generic AI-generated art website.

It should feel:

-   Japanese;
-   quiet;
-   sophisticated;
-   emotional;
-   artistic;
-   cinematic;
-   spacious;
-   editorial;
-   timeless.

The visitor should feel that they are entering a world rather than
opening a database.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. DESIGN REFERENCES

### teamLab Borderless

Borrow the **philosophy of experience**, not the visual style. The
visitor should feel immersed in a world.

### Major museum archives / Tate-like discipline

Borrow structured cataloguing, preservation discipline, metadata,
relationships, and contextual depth.

### Japanese museum design

Borrow restraint, negative space, typography, silence, controlled
transitions, and respect for the artwork.

### National Geographic-like storytelling

Use visual storytelling to connect **place → person → experience →
artwork → memory**.

### Taschen / David Annesley's *Kurumidza*

Use premium editorial composition, warm paper-like tones, generous
yohaku, elegant typography, and the feeling of a physical art book.

### The Tsuji family

This is the element that cannot be copied: the home, walls, family,
children, artist, and paintings living together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. GOLDEN RULES

> **Never let technology become more visible than the paintings.**

> **Never turn TSUJI WORLD into a generic AI-generated art website.**

> **The artwork is the interface. The archive is the foundation. The
> family is the soul.**

AI may assist with implementation, search, indexing, translation
support, image processing, archive organisation, and workflow. AI must
not invent artwork facts, quotations, family memories, exhibition
history, provenance, or art-historical claims.

# 53. SOURCE AND FACT DISCIPLINE

The archive contains multiple evidence types. The system must not
collapse them into one undifferentiated truth layer.

Conceptual evidence types:

``` text
VERIFIED / OFFICIAL RECORD
        ↓
ARTIST VOICE
        ↓
FAMILY RECORD / MEMORY
        ↓
EXPERT COMMENTARY
        ↓
STUDENT MEMORY
        ↓
CURATORIAL INTERPRETATION
        ↓
UNVERIFIED / TO BE VERIFIED
```

This is **not a ranking of artistic value**. It is a distinction in
evidence type and provenance.
